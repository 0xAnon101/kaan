# Kaan privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Kaan is a hearing budget app published by Dronzer. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

Kaan collects no personal data. Everything it records stays on your phone. Kaan has no account, no server and no analytics.

## What Kaan reads on your phone

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Media and call volume level | To estimate the sound level at your ear | Stays on your phone |
| Which audio output is in use and its name, for example the name of your earphones | To count earphones and ignore speakers | Stays on your phone |
| Whether audio is playing, and whether an app marks it as speech | To count listening time | Stays on your phone |
| Precise mode only: how strong the audio signal is that your phone sends to your earphones | To make the estimate closer | Used for a moment in memory, then discarded |

Kaan never opens the microphone and never listens to the sounds around you. It does not read your messages, contacts, location or files.

## Precise mode and the record audio permission

Precise mode is off unless you switch it on. It reads one number several times a second: the loudness of the audio your phone is playing. Android protects that reading with the "record audio" permission, so Android shows its standard microphone prompt. Kaan does not record, save or send any audio. It keeps only the resulting dose figures described below. You can switch precise mode off in Kaan's settings or remove the permission in Android settings at any time.

## What Kaan stores

Kaan stores your settings, your list of earphones and a daily summary of estimated listening (dose, time, average level, loudest level). It keeps daily summaries for 90 days and then deletes them. All of it sits in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling Kaan, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything.

## What Kaan shares

Nothing. Kaan sends no data to Dronzer or to any third party.

## Purchases

Subscriptions to Kaan Pro go through Google Play. Google handles the payment and holds the payment details. Kaan only learns from Google Play whether a subscription is active on your account. Google's own privacy policy covers that processing.

## Health information

Kaan is a wellness guide. It estimates sound exposure from your volume setting. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat any condition, and its figures are not medical measurements. See an audiologist or doctor if you notice ringing, pain or muffled hearing.

## Children

Kaan collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
